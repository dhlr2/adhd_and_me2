# ADHD and Me

**A supportive Android companion for people newly diagnosed with ADHD.**

## About the App

**ADHD and Me** is designed specifically for individuals who have recently been diagnosed with ADHD. The goal is simple: **help you understand your brain better, build better habits, and feel less alone** on this journey.

Whether you're struggling with focus, trying to learn more about ADHD, or just need a gentle way to organize your day — this app is built with empathy and care.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Focus Tools
- Simple and effective task-focused timer
- Pick one task at a time to reduce overwhelm
- Helps you start and stay focused using a clean, distraction-free interface

### 2. Learn ADHD
- Easy-to-understand explanations of the most common ADHD symptoms
- Each symptom includes:
  - Clear description
  - Real-life examples
  - "What Helps" section with practical tips

### 3. Daily Planner
- Add, edit, and organize your daily tasks
- Mark tasks as complete with satisfaction
- Delete or archive tasks easily
- Built with ADHD-friendly design (simple, not overwhelming)

### 4. Community
- Curated list of helpful external resources:
  - Trusted websites
  - Active Reddit communities
  - ADHD charities and support organizations
  - Educational and motivational YouTube channels

## Who is this app for?

- People who are **newly diagnosed** with ADHD
- Anyone who wants a **gentle, non-judgmental** productivity tool
- Individuals looking to better understand how their ADHD brain works
- Users who prefer simple, clean interfaces over complex feature-heavy apps

## Tech Stack

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **IDE**: Android Studio
- **Architecture**: MVVM 
- **UI**: Material Design 3 / Jetpack Compose
